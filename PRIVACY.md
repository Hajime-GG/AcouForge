# AcouForge Privacy

**Last updated: October 2026**

AcouForge is designed to perform audio processing locally on your Windows computer.

## Local Processing

Audio processed by AcouForge is handled locally on your computer. AcouForge does not require uploading your audio to a remote server for normal audio processing.

## Settings and Profiles

AcouForge stores application settings, calibration profiles, and target presets locally on your Windows system.

These files are stored under:

```text
%LOCALAPPDATA%\AcouForge
